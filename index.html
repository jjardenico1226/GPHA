CCTV Access

How to install & view the Street CCTV

Follow these steps to set up the Hik-Connect app on your phone. Once your account is approved, you'll be able to view our street cameras live.

Sign up via the mobile app

  1. Download Hik-Connect (or "Hik-Connect for end user") from the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Samsung Galaxy Store.
  2. Open the app, tap Login, then tap Register.
  3. Select your country/region and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  4. Enter your email address, or switch to register by phone number, then request the security/verification code.
  5. Enter the code sent to your email (check your spam folder if needed) or phone, create a strong password (at least 8 characters, using at least three of: uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters), then tap Finish.
  6. Scan this QR code using the in-app scanner, verification code GPHA2026:
  7. Message Jeffrey Jardenico on Messenger for approval — scanning the code only sends a request; he still has to approve it in the app before you're let in.
  8. Once approved, tap on any of the 7 CCTV cameras in your app and enter the device password he provides.
  9. You'll now be able to view our Street CCTV live.

A note on the device password in step 8: that one isn't posted here, since unlike the QR code above, it has no approval step of its own — whoever has it can view the stream directly. Jeffrey shares it only after approving your request, so access still stays with vetted residents.

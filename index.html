Welcome to a Community That Cares

For generations, we have proudly served the homeowners of G. Pedro Street, fostering connection, progress, and a place everyone is proud to call home.

A neighborhood association working with Barangay New Zaniga to keep our streets safe, clean, and well looked after — from security and lighting to drainage and community support.
